ABOUT

PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

Research driven by science, focused on solutions.

I am a Researcher at the National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN), Indonesia. My academic qualifications include a doctoral degree (Dr.) from IPB University, a Master of Science (M.Sc.) from Yeungnam University, a forestry degree (S.Hut.) from Winaya Mukti University, and a Diploma from IPB University. My research interests span materials science, porous carbon materials, and energy storage technologies.

My research interests include the design and development of porous carbon materials, activated carbon processing, nanomaterials, and electrochemical energy storage. This website brings together my research themes, selected projects, scientific outputs, and opportunities for academic and industrial collaboration.
